# Motion Lab

Original experiments on the portfolio. Fog and glass on black, Google Sans, one calm mint accent. Each tile keeps its own gesture. Nothing plays the frame for you.

## Grid

Tiles are 16:10. The first tile spans both columns. Each card holds an iframe laid out at its own stage size and scaled with `transform` so the subject sits centered. Fullscreen is the viewport: the experiment fills everything except a 360px panel on the right. On a narrow screen the panel is a collapsed peek. Close sits at the top-right. Esc closes it.

## Motion

Springs only, interruptible gestures, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Scroll studies pin inside the iframe so the page itself does not scroll. Visuals are CSS and SVG. No stock photos.
