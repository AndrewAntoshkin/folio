About

section/02

I have spent most of my career solving systemic problems inside product teams. What interests me is not just designing interfaces, but improving how they get made — through design systems, code, AI, and automation.

Today I grow the design system at Yandex HR Tech — from component libraries and tokens to documentation, code, and AI practices in the team's daily work. Before that I worked on design systems at Sber and designed products at Otkritie Broker and MTS.

AI became part of that process. I design in Figma, write code, and build my own products with Claude, Cursor, and other tools — not as an experiment, but to test ideas faster and get them to a working state.

Hi.
I'm Andrew Antoshkin, a designer. I make products, design systems, and tools that tie design and code together.

I'm not just interested in designing interfaces — I want to shape how they get made: through design systems, tooling, code, and automation. Especially now, when AI is changing not only individual tasks but the way we work on products at all.

Alongside that I build my own projects and open‑source tools for designers and engineers. What excites me most are tools that let both sides work from a single spec. Among them — ds‑health, figma‑to‑design‑md, design‑system‑ai‑starter, spektr, ds‑lint, and ds‑coverage.
